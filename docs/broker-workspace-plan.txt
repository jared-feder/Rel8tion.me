Product direction
The broker workspace sits above authorized agents in one office. Its first job is to make the broker's weekly supervision useful: see what happened, find the evidence, resolve exceptions, and move responsibility without losing relationships. Competition and recognition encourage participation; they should grow from reliable operating data. No pricing, paid entitlement, public ranking or production-data contract is changed by this work.

New York compliance research -> product requirements (reviewed Nov 6, 2026)
This is a workflow baseline, not a certification of legal compliance or a complete transaction-management checklist. Confirm additional state, brokerage, association, MLS, transaction and insurer obligations with the broker's counsel before publication. The user has not yet confirmed jurisdictions; New York follows the Cedarhurst example.

A. Agency relationship: RPL 443 addresses applicable residential disclosure timing, acknowledgement/copy delivery and minimum three-year retention. Refusal requires the prescribed sworn/affirmed factual declaration, not a manufactured signature. Implement separate delivered, signed, refused-with-declaration, missing and needs-review states; preserve declared agency and source evidence.
Source: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/443

B. 19 NYCRR 175.21 calls for regular broker supervision and identifiable listing/transaction records. 175.23 specifies three-year recordkeeping for covered transactions, including relevant agreements. 175.28 governs fair-housing notice delivery and evidence retention, including electronic copies and refusal declarations. 175.29 includes office/website and open-house posting/availability requirements. Product: exception review queue, evidence export, dated supervision notes, transaction-file links, and an on-site posting checklist. A digital checkbox cannot prove the physical poster was displayed. Set retention by document type and applicable trigger; counsel must settle policy and legal holds, rather than auto-deleting every file exactly three years after check-in.
Official DOS law/rules source: https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-license-law (sections 175.21, 175.23, 175.28, 175.29)

C. RPL 442-h(4) requires broker SOPs addressing specified buyer-service prerequisites, with dating/notarization, public site/app posting, availability and change-history obligations. DOS explains that filing a copy with the Department was removed. Product: approved office policy version, publication targets, effective dates, agent acknowledgements and update checks. Uploading a file alone does not establish notarization or legal approval.
Sources: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/442-H
https://dos.ny.gov/standardized-operating-procedures

D. Distinguish law from association rules. NAR's open-house guide says simply attending an open house without an agent does not require a written buyer agreement under its described policy. Product: no automatic conversion from visitor sign-in to exclusive representation. Buyer-agency agreements, marketing permission and financing consent require their own applicable workflow.
Source: https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/consumer-guide-to-open-houses-and-written-agreements

E. Advertising: DOS describes listing-agent attribution and paid-advertisement disclosures for applicable third-party advertising. Product: public cards/listing promotion retain correct broker/listing identity and approved required notices. These are separate from an internal event attendance leaderboard.
Source: https://dos.ny.gov/real-estate-advertising

Immediate production blockers / first real rollout sequence
1. Protect the existing signed-record API before expanding access. The inspected ny-disclosure handler has no visible session/agent/broker authorization check and uses service-role reads when given a check-in ID. Its GET download can regenerate/store a packet. No live exploit or real buyer document retrieval was attempted. Add server-verified agent/broker/compliance access, event/organization authorization and appropriate scoped consumer receipt access; preserve public unsigned form preview. Make archival reads non-mutating, and make any regeneration an explicit authorized audited operation preserving earlier artifacts. Deploy only after separate review and production authorization.
2. Add authoritative organization, office and historical affiliation mappings on existing universal organizations/workspaces/memberships/domain assignments. Never grant access by matching a brokerage display-name string or user-editable metadata. Separate broker admin, office manager/compliance read, agent own scope and LO consent scope. Joining a new firm must not automatically reveal all prior-firm history.
3. Durable private evidence storage: exact template bytes/hash, form version and effective policy snapshot, check-in/event/buyer/agent linkage, e-sign intent/consent evidence, timestamps and delivery records, immutable revisions, access/download log, retention/legal holds, encrypted backup/recovery and searchable metadata. At closeout enqueue an idempotent archiving job keyed by event/check-in/form version; failure stays visible and retryable without resending the recap or closing an event twice. Broker exports include an evidence manifest and authorized originals, with missing files explicitly listed. No fabricated or regenerated original.
4. Form lifecycle: draft upload -> file validation/scanning -> field/schema mapping -> broker/legal approval -> office/state/transaction applicability -> future effective version -> visible buyer review and separately applicable acknowledgement/signature -> immutable archived receipt. Do not embed arbitrary executable PDF content or allow upload to silently replace required state forms. Provide accessible language/assistance routes and refusal handling approved by counsel.
5. Real roster membership/invitation and lead transfer: verified identities, server-owned membership roles, explicit office scope, audited before/after, offboarding handoff and session revocation. Route transfer of represented clients through documented authorization/review; do not broaden marketing/LO consent or transfer financial application data. Preserve original agent credit and separate current responsibility.
6. Connect broker-scoped read adapters and paginate full reporting data; never reuse the current limited agent list as a complete brokerage universe. Reconcile canonical check-in/disclosure status logic. Broker counts must distinguish scheduled, held, cancelled, incomplete setup and no recorded attendance. Publish data freshness, unavailable-source warnings and date scope.

Analytics that are useful enough to sell
Weekend brief -> office -> agent -> property -> event -> evidence. Show total hosting agents; scheduled/held/cancelled events; held events with check-ins (11/12 = 91.7%); visits versus deduplicated identities; median attendance per held event including zeros; unique new relationships versus represented visitors; missing-record exceptions; follow-up eligibility/review/response and handoff counts when real channels exist. Give conversion stages explicit denominators and attribution windows; check-in is not an exclusive lead, a qualified buyer or a closed sale. Track listing-agent and host contributions separately. Add week-over-week changes only when coverage is comparable. Exports should support seller reports and broker meetings without exposing the visitor roster publicly.

Gamification and awards roadmap
Office-first, optional weekly/monthly challenges: consistent hosting, verified follow-through, teamwork and personal improvement. Keep activity, recognition and verified closed production separate; use appropriate office/market/time/tenure cohorts. Display how each rank is calculated; handle ties, corrections, duplicate/self/test activity and stale feeds. Avoid an opaque paid-placement score.
Buyer/seller/peer recognition: one verified interaction can nominate a narrowly described badge such as Great communicator or Helpful teammate. Relationship type, date window, duplicate control, moderation, appeals and optional public display are required. Do not expose buyer identities or permit anonymous fabricated endorsements. An achievement badge reflects its published rule; it is not a state certification or a guarantee of results.
Global production: only with an authorized, licensed MLS/broker/CRM source, defined closed-date window, sides versus transactions, team/member attribution rules, market scope and correction process. Self-reported numbers must be labeled separately from verified data. Do not scrape or invent external production. Broker permission does not substitute for the data provider's license. Public leaderboard/privacy choices are a later product release, not enabled here.

Suggested release order
First: protected evidence archive + exception queue + trusted office/event analytics.
Second: complete roster/lead handoff, shared event/listing calendar and approved custom-form capture.
Third: office challenges, private milestones and moderated recognition.
Fourth: licensed verified production comparison and optional public/global profiles.
Commercial hypothesis: brokers buy operating visibility, less document chasing and accountable follow-up; agents use it because check-in and work capture are easy, with recognition as a bonus. Measure time to first live event, weekly active offices/agents, complete evidence coverage, missing-record resolution time and verified follow-up outcomes before claiming savings or revenue gains.

Verification and boundaries
Focused unit/API tests cover derived totals, zeros/repeats, range and DST boundaries, tenant/role isolation, origin/consent preservation, representation blocking, roster history, audit trail, form draft validation/publication hold, unchanged historical evidence, API CSRF and deployment guard. Existing disclosure PDF, weekly production archive, universal auth and buyer AI tests run alongside them. Browser walkthrough and screenshot evidence are recorded in the completion handoff. No claim of production authentication, real signed PDFs, state compliance certification, live custom forms, badges, MLS feed or global leaderboard is made.
