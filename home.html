How It Works

Built for the live open-house floor.

Step 1

Tap to Activate

The agent arrives, scans or taps, confirms the detected listing when needed, and launches the event in about 20 seconds.

Step 2

Check In Buyers

Visitors check in from the Smart Sign or Event Pass. No app, no password, no account, no required email, and no clipboard.

Step 3

Sign and Store

Rel8tion guides required acknowledgements, captures verifiable e-signed disclosure records, and keeps the event paperless.

Step 4

Work the Dashboard

The agent sees check-ins, disclosures, event recap, and next-step actions.

Step 5

Route Help

When a buyer asks for financing help, the request can route privately to available loan officer support.
